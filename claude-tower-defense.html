How to play

  • Buy monkeys from the shop on the right, then click the grass to place them. They can't go on the track, in water, or on buildings, trees and rocks.
  • Start a round and balloons float along the track. Every layer you pop pays $1, and each cleared round pays a bonus.
  • Balloons that escape cost lives, one life for every layer still inside them. Run out of lives and the game ends.
  • Click a tower to upgrade it. Every tower has three paths of five tiers: one path can go to 5, one more to 2, the third stays empty. Towers sell for 50%. Subs and Buccaneers go on water; the Heli follows your mouse, the Dartling fires at it, and the Mortar shells a spot you pick.
  • Click a map to pick a mode. Clearing a mode unlocks the next one: Easy leads to Primary Only and Deflation, Intermediate to Reverse and Apopalypse, and Hard to Double HP, Half Cash, Impoppable and CHIMPS.
  • Tunnels hide balloons. Nothing can hit them while they're inside.
